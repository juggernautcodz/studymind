## Short Description (80 characters max)

## Full Description (4000 characters max)

StudyMind is your intelligent study companion that transforms the way you learn. Record your lectures and let AI automatically create comprehensive study materials tailored to your courses.

KEY FEATURES:

SMART LECTURE RECORDING
Record lectures directly in the app and get AI-powered transcriptions. Never miss important information again.

AI-GENERATED STUDY MATERIALS
Automatically generate organized notes, flashcards, and practice quizzes from your recordings. Study smarter, not harder.

MIND MAP VISUALIZATION
See your courses and topics as interactive mind maps. Understand connections between concepts at a glance.

WHITEBOARD & NOTES CAPTURE
Snap photos of whiteboards, textbooks, or handwritten notes. Our OCR technology extracts text and creates study materials instantly.

ORGANIZED LEARNING
Keep everything tidy with our hierarchical structure:
- Semesters to organize your academic year
- Courses within each semester
- Topics to break down each course

SPACED REPETITION
Master your flashcards with our scientifically-proven spaced repetition algorithm. The app tracks your progress and optimizes review timing.

MULTI-LANGUAGE SUPPORT
Study in your preferred language! StudyMind supports 12 languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, and Russian.

EXPORT & SHARE
Export your notes and flashcards as PDFs. Share study materials with classmates easily.

STUDY REMINDERS
Set personalized notifications to maintain your study streak and never forget a review session.

Perfect for:
- University students
- High school students  
- Professional learners
- Anyone who wants to study more efficiently

Download StudyMind today and revolutionize your learning experience!

## Account Deletion
Users can delete all their data from within the app:
Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete My Data

Users can also delete their account via:
Settings > DATA > Delete Account

A server-side endpoint (DELETE /api/auth/data) handles complete data removal including all user content, study materials, and personal information.
