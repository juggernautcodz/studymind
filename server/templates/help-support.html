Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create flashcards or study notes?

You can record a lecture, upload an audio file, take photos of textbook pages/notes, or paste text directly. StudyMind's AI automatically processes your content into structured notes, flashcards, and practice quizzes.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play. Open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → select StudyMind → tap Cancel subscription.

How do I delete my data or account?

You can delete your data or account directly inside the app under Settings → DATA / Privacy & Data. Alternatively, email us at studymindv1@hotmail.com and we will process your deletion request within 30 days.
